Open TravelExplorer for iPhone & iPad

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, open oscarplaysroblox.github.io/travel-explorer/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open TravelExplorer from your Home Screen. It opens full screen, like a normal app.

Works best on iOS / iPadOS 15.4 or newer. It also works in any browser on Android, Mac and PC.

System requirements
