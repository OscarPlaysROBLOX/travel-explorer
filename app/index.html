    About TravelExplorer

    Update 1: TravelExplorer for iPhone & iPad. Made for Galaxy.

    Countries from the REST Countries dataset (v3.1 data file, gitlab.com/restcountries/restcountries), downloaded Oct 2026. Population figures are as listed in that dataset. Flags: flagcdn.com images, bundled with the app.

    US States: capital, largest city, population (2020 U.S. Census) and total area (U.S. Census Bureau) from Wikipedia's "List of states and territories of the United States" (Oct 2026). Nicknames and time zones are commonly cited values.

    After the first visit, all data is saved on your device so the app works offline. The only thing that goes online is the "Open in map" button, which opens Apple Maps.

    Install on iPhone or iPad

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    No account, no ads, no tracking.
