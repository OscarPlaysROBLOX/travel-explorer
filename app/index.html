    TravelExplorer

    Made by Galaxy. Look up countries and US states. After the first visit everything is stored on this device, so it works offline. Only the “Open in map” button goes online.

    On iPad or iPhone: tap the Share button in Safari, then “Add to Home Screen” to get an app icon.

    Data sources

      Facts change over time (populations especially). Double-check important details before your trip.

      Favorites are saved only on this device (in Safari's website storage). No account, no ads, no tracking.
